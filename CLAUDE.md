# Arrington Business Consultancy Prototype

## What this is

Single-page prototype website for Arrington Business Consultancy (Tom Arrington). Static HTML deployed to Railway.

## Live URLs

- **Current (V2):** https://arrington-prototype-production.up.railway.app
- **V1 (preserved, no public link):** /v1.html (warm palette, "We" voice, Outward Mindset approach)

## Tech stack

- Single HTML file (`index.html`), no framework
- Served via `npx serve` on Railway
- Fonts: Google Fonts (DM Sans, DM Serif Display)
- No build step, no dependencies beyond `serve`

## Voice and tone

- **First person** ("I", not "We") throughout
- **British English** only (programme, organise, colour)
- UK business language: VAT, the books, fixed overheads, cash flow, gross margin
- Direct, dry, blunt. No AI clichés (unlocking, empowering, seamless, transformative, journey, potential, synergy)
- No em dashes

## Key content

- **Hero:** "Nineteen years of operational grit. Oxford-backed strategy. A million-pound exit."
- **Biography:** Abacus and Falmouth Taxis (real story, clutch/flywheel, fifteenfold revenue, million-pound exit)
- **Case study:** Orca Marine (VAT mess, books reorganised, professional footing)
- **CTA:** "We start with a coffee. If I cannot add significant value, I will not take your money."
