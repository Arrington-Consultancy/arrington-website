Case study

Orca Marine

A sinking ship. High turnover masked a mess underneath.

The mess

Turnover looked healthy on paper. Behind it: no budgeting discipline, VAT in a state, creditors chasing, and nobody with a clear picture of what the business actually owed. The books were not wrong. They were ignored.

The steady hand

I went through every line. Reorganised the books from scratch. Got the VAT sorted. Ring-fenced liabilities. Put spending controls in place that the team could actually follow. Set up proper reporting so the directors could see where they stood each week, not each quarter.

The result

The business went from chaos to a professional footing. Creditors were paid down on terms. The directors could make decisions based on real numbers. It stopped being a crisis and started being a business again.

Honest questions

Before you ring anyone, ask yourself these

Not a quiz. Not a sales funnel. Just the questions I would ask you over a coffee.

Do you know your actual cost per unit, per job, or per hour billed?
Could you take three weeks off and come back to a business that ran without you?
When did you last cancel a subscription or service you no longer use?
Is your VAT up to date and filed on time, every time?
Can you tell me your gross margin this month without looking it up?
If you sold tomorrow, what would a buyer actually be buying?
