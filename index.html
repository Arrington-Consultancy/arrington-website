Case study

Rescue and exit

A real intervention. Details anonymised to protect the parties involved.

The crisis

Business in terminal decline. Leadership had failed at every level. Operational accounts at zero. Creditors circling. Staff morale destroyed. The board had no plan and no credibility left with the market.

The intervention

I assumed total financial control. Ring-fenced all liabilities. Implemented immediate spending discipline across every department. Renegotiated terms with key creditors. Stripped out every cost that wasn't directly protecting revenue or cash flow.

The result

Two years of hands-on management rebuilt market trust, stabilised the operation, and restored financial credibility. The business was taken to a successful exit. Stakeholders recovered their position. Staff kept their jobs.

Self-assessment

Am I trapped?

Answer honestly. If three or more of these are true, your business has a structural problem. Not a you problem.

Could your business run for a month without you being involved day to day?
Do you know, right now, which of your products or services is most profitable?
When did you last take a full week off without checking in?
If your best employee left tomorrow, is their knowledge documented anywhere?
Are your financial reports telling you what to do, or just what happened?
Do you spend more time working in the business than on it?
