1How each line was judged

Every line was asked three questions, in this order.

  1. Is it true and does it keep the rules? A fact the page cannot back up, first person outside Useful Thinking, a dash or hyphen, a banned word, anything that implies the owner's business is broken. These are marked Fact or Brand rule.
  2. Would Tom say it? Your CORE voice rules: direct, plain, conversational, medium-length sentences, no groups of three for effect, no stacked fragments, no AI mannerisms. Marked Style.
  3. Does changing it help the brand? The test from the Brand OS: does it make a serious owner more likely to trust us and get in touch? If a line already works, it stays, even if I could write it differently. Marked Commercial where the change is about winning the enquiry.

Change means I recommend it. Your call means either version is defensible and it comes down to taste. Keep lines are listed under each page so you can see they were read, not skipped.

Left alone on purpose: client reviews (they are speech, published as written), the fifteen Useful Thinking articles (your own approved first-person writing; there is no tested voice track for them yet, so the voice rules say they are published exactly as you approved them), the start-up page (frozen during the Ads test), prices and offer names, and the Privacy page.
