AI WORKERS

Six workers, one office.

Each one does a single job. None of them sends, books or posts anything on its own.

Bookings

Reads booking emails and checks each one against the job in dispatch: times, names, phone numbers, flight numbers, and anything booked twice. Gets the change ready for dispatch and drafts the reply.

Checker

A second AI that checks every booking against the customer's own email and shows anything that doesn't match. Nothing goes into dispatch until someone in the office signs it off.

Customer emails

Answers everything else customers send, from lost property to account questions, and looks up the journey when the answer is in dispatch.

Complaints

Logs every complaint and pulls the job from dispatch, so the reply rests on what happened. Passes anything about safety to Licensing.

Licensing

Keeps every badge, plate, MOT and insurance date in one register, flags what is due and drafts the letter to the council.

Social media

Writes the week's posts from what is going on at the firm. Nothing is posted until the owner approves it.

BOOKINGS

A booking checked against dispatch.

Paul asked for a 5.30am pickup to the airport. Dispatch had 5.50. The AI reads the email, then plain code compares it with the job, field by field, and shows the difference. One click puts it right in dispatch, and the reply is drafted ready for someone to check.

A booking email beside what dispatch holds. The reply is as the AI drafted it.

COMPLAINTS

A complaint answered from the record.

Ruth's car was 16 minutes late and she missed her train. The worker pulled the job from dispatch: a road closure at Exton, and a controller's note that nobody rang her. So the draft says what happened, owns the part that was the firm's fault, and leaves any refund to the office.
